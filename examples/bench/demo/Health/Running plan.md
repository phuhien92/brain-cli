# Running plan

Training for a half marathon in October.

## Week shape

- Tuesday: intervals, six times 800 metres.
- Thursday: an easy forty minutes.
- **Sunday long run**: starts at 10 km and grows by 1 km a week to 18 km, three weeks
  before the race, then tapers.

## Gear

New shoes every 600 km. Log runs in the [[Weekly review template]].
