# Packing list

## Always

Passport, chargers, a universal adapter, medication, a reusable water bottle.

## City trip

Comfortable walking shoes, one smart outfit, a light rain jacket.

## Beach trip

Sunscreen, a hat, swimwear, sandals, a quick-dry towel, a book, a dry bag for the phone.

## Hiking trip

Layers, a first-aid kit, a headlamp, snacks, a map downloaded for offline use.
