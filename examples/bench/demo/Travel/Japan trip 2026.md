# Japan trip 2026

Two weeks in November: Tokyo, Kyoto, Kanazawa, back to Tokyo.

## Rail pass

Decided **not** to buy the national rail pass. After the price increase it only pays off
with two long trips a week; our route has one Tokyo–Kyoto leg and one Kyoto–Kanazawa leg.
Buy single shinkansen tickets instead and use an IC card for city trains.

## Stays

- Tokyo: a hotel near the station, four nights.
- Kyoto: a ryokan for two nights (not booked yet), then a hotel.
- Kanazawa: two nights.
