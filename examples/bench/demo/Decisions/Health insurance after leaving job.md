# Health insurance after leaving my job

## Options

- **COBRA**: keep the employer plan for up to 18 months, paying the full premium plus 2%.
  Same doctors, same deductible already met this year. About 780 a month.
- **Marketplace plan**: a silver plan with a slightly narrower network. About 410 a month
  after the premium tax credit, which depends on this year's income.
- **Spouse's plan**: not available; the open enrollment window is in November.

## Decision

Take the marketplace silver plan, starting the first of the month after the last day of
work, so there is no gap. Keep COBRA as the fallback: it can be elected up to 60 days
after coverage ends and is retroactive, so it covers a surprise bill in the gap.

Dr Patel (dentist) is in network for both. Re-check the tax credit estimate in the
[[Budget 2026]] when income changes.
