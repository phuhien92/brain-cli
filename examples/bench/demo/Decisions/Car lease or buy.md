# Car: lease or buy

The current car's lease ends in August.

## Lease

Lower monthly payment, a new car every three years, but a 10,000-mile yearly limit and
nothing owned at the end. The drive to the trailhead every weekend pushes us over the limit.

## Buy

A two-year-old hybrid, paid partly from savings and partly with a 4-year loan. Higher
payment for four years, then nothing. Insurance is about the same.

## Decision

**Buy** a used hybrid. Mileage is the deciding factor, and keeping a car for eight years is
cheaper than three leases. Keep the emergency fund untouched; the down payment comes from
the car savings line in [[Budget 2026]].
