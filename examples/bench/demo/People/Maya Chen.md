# Maya Chen

Mentor since 2023; previously led the platform team where I started.

## How to stay in touch

Maya prefers **email** over chat, and a short written agenda before any call. We meet
once a month, usually the last Thursday, for thirty minutes.

## What she has taught me

- Write the decision first, then the reasons.
- A plan nobody reads is not a plan.
- Ask for the review before you think you are ready.

## Notes from recent calls

- Suggested practising system design out loud for [[Interview prep - frontend]].
- Recommended keeping a [[Weekly review template]] instead of a daily journal.
