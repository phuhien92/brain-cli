# Weeknight dal

Thirty minutes, one pot, feeds four.

## Ingredients

Red lentils (one cup), an onion, garlic, ginger, a tin of tomatoes, turmeric, cumin seeds,
a handful of spinach, lemon.

## Method

1. Rinse the lentils and simmer them in three cups of water with turmeric for twenty minutes.
2. Meanwhile, fry the onion, then garlic and ginger, then the tomatoes until thick.
3. Stir the tomato mix into the lentils with the spinach.
4. Finish with cumin seeds fried in hot oil (a tarka) and a squeeze of lemon.
