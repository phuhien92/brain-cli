# Sourdough starter

The starter lives in the fridge in the tall jar.

## Feeding

Feed it **once a week** when it lives in the fridge, and twice a day when it lives on the
counter before a bake. Ratio 1:5:5 by weight — one part starter, five parts flour, five
parts water — keeps it from getting too sour.

## Before baking

Take it out two days ahead and feed it morning and evening until it doubles within six
hours.
