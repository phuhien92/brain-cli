# Garden Plan

## Beds

Two raised beds, 4 by 8 feet, cedar, along the south fence where the sun lasts longest.
Fill with a mix of topsoil and compost; budget for soil is in [[Budget 2026]].

## What to plant

- Tomatoes: two cherry, two San Marzano for sauce.
- Basil between the tomatoes.
- Lettuce and radishes early, before the tomatoes need the space.
- Herbs in pots by the kitchen door so they survive the [[Kitchen Renovation]].

## Watering

Drip line on a timer: twenty minutes every morning in summer, every other day in spring.
Check the timer battery when the clocks change.
