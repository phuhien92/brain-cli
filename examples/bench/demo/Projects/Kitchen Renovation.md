# Kitchen Renovation

The kitchen gets new cabinets, counters and lighting this year. Budget: 18,000, with a
10% contingency held back in the [[Budget 2026]] home line.

## Contractor

Three quotes. We chose Alder & Sons: not the cheapest, but the only one who could start
before the summer and who answered every email within a day. Final quote due next week.

## Countertops

Decision: **quartz** in a warm white, honed finish. Marble was the first choice but it
stains and etches; butcher block needs oiling every month. Quartz costs a little more than
laminate and needs nothing. Samples are in the drawer by the back door.

## Lighting

Under-cabinet LED strips on one switch, two pendants over the island. The electrician
needs the cabinet drawings first.

## Timeline

1. Demolition — first week of June.
2. Cabinets — two weeks.
3. Countertops — template after cabinets, install ten days later.
4. Paint and lighting.
